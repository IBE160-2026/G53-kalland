- (technique by coach) started Worst Possible Idea — invent the worst rule, flip it into a rule plus a test row

- (idea by coach) Two entitlement sources: live visibility grant (free) vs permanent purchase grant
- (idea by coach) Worst: price raise charges earlier buyers. Flip: purchase price is frozen on the purchase row

- (insight by coach) Core model: org is the unit of access, ownership and history; the user is the unit of login and audit
- (insight by coach) Two entitlement sources — live visibility grant (free, follows current visibility) and permanent purchase grant (survives visibility/price change, archiving, contact deactivation)
- (insight by coach) Assets and orgs are archived/deactivated, never hard-deleted once purchases exist — this single rule resolves most edge cases
- (insight by coach) No email in v1 is solved by single-use setup links shown to the admin; the same token upgrades to emailed links in stage 2
- (insight by coach) All access decisions go through one module (canSee, canDownloadOriginal, canBuy) and a truth table in the PRD, mirrored row by row in tests
