Logged. Before we start the sections, here is my take on the size limit, plus two conflicts with earlier decisions that you need to settle.

**Maximum file size: I'd suggest 50 MB per file.** Full-frame cameras with 45–61 MP sensors produce JPEGs of roughly 15–40 MB at the highest quality, so 50 MB covers them with some room to spare.
