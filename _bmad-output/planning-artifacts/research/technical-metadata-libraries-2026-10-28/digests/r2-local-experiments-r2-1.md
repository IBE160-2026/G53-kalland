# Digest R2 — Local experiments and follow-up checks (round 2)

Lead-following on digests A, B and C. Run by the lead on 2026-10-28 on macOS 26.5.2 (Apple Silicon), exiftool 13.55 (Homebrew), Node 26.7.0, Python 3.14.7. Test file: a JPEG made with `sips`, then tagged with exiftool with GPS in EXIF (GPS IFD) and XMP (`XMP-exif:GPSLatitude/Longitude`), and the city in `IPTC:City`, `XMP-photoshop:City` and `XMP-iptcExt:LocationShownCity`. Scripts and outputs live in the session scratchpad and are not committed. The commands are reproduced below.
