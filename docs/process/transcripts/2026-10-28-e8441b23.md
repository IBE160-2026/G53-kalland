**Two separate actions**

| Action | Orgs with a licence | Orgs without one |
|---|---|---|
| **Change sharing** (the everyday action) | Keep access | Lose access |
| **Revoke access** (the exception) | Lose access, after a warning | Lose access |

Revoke can target one org or everyone, which covers "remove the image entirely".

**The revoke workflow**
1. The admin chooses **Revoke access**, either on the image page or on an org's client card.
2. A warning lists the affected orgs and their licences: who acquired it, when, and at what price.
3. The admin must give a reason: lost rights, shared by mistake, person in the image, or other with a free-text note.
4. The admin confirms with **Remove anyway**.
5. The licence row is **marked revoked** with a date, the admin and the reason. It is **not deleted**, so the history and invoicing stay correct.
6. A revoked paid licence is flagged in the admin's purchase overview as "may need a credit". The credit itself is handled manually, outside the app, in v1.
7. The image disappears from the client's "Mine bilder". The client's licence history shows it as "Removed by the agency". It doesn't silently vanish, which would only lead to confused phone calls.

"Remove entirely" is the same flow with every org selected, followed by archiving the image. Images that no one has a licence for can be deleted outright, as before.
